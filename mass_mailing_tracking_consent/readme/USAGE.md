1.  Install the module.
2.  Make sure the privacy notice mentions the tracking pixel, its purposes
    and the retention period.
3.  Collect the tracking consent, for example through a website form, a
    contract, a newsletter subscription or an import, and record it in
    *Email Marketing > Configuration > Tracking Consents* or directly on the
    contact/partner (*Consent to Email Tracking* field).
4.  Check the *Tracking Privacy* tab of the mailing. Keep the default policy
    for ordinary promotional mailings; use *Never* or *Always (exemptions
    only)* only when the legal basis requires it.
5.  Send the mailing as usual. Recipients without a granted consent receive
    the same email without the tracking pixel and without tracked links: the
    short links are replaced by their target URL, in HTML and plain text.
6.  Keep the footer of the mailing, which contains the unsubscribe link and,
    for mailings under the consent policy, the tracking preferences link.

Withdrawing the tracking consent only stops the tracking: the recipient keeps
receiving the mailings without the pixel. To stop the emails as well, the
recipient uses the unsubscribe link.
