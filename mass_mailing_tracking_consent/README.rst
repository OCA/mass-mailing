This module makes mass mailings comply with the Italian rules on
tracking pixels in email, from the point of view of the Odoo deployer.

Odoo 18 always adds a per-recipient tracking pixel to mass mailings and
tracks link clicks per recipient. It has no per-recipient switch, so a
mailing is either fully tracked or not sent. This is not compatible with
the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante privacy) guidelines on
tracking pixels in email communications (Provvedimento n. 284 of 17
April 2026, published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 98 of 29 April 2026),
which require prior, informed and specific consent and allow the
recipient to withdraw only the tracking consent while continuing to
receive the emails.

With this module:

- tracking is disabled by default; a pixel and tracked links are
  included only when the recipient granted a tracking consent;
- the recipient's choice is stored per email address, with date, source,
  originating mailing and an audit trail in the chatter;
- the consent is directly editable and searchable on mailing contacts
  and partners, so it can be used in mailing domains, filters and
  imports;
- every mailing has its own tracking policy: only consenting recipients
  (default), never, or always for communications covered by a legal
  exemption;
- every tracked or non-tracked mailing can contain a footer link to a
  public page where the recipient can give or withdraw the tracking
  consent, or unsubscribe from all emails;
- the footer link is protected by an opaque HMAC token; the email
  address is not included in the URL;
- when tracking is denied, the core pixel, the OCA ``mail_tracking``
  pixel and the per-recipient click suffix are removed from both the
  HTML and the plain-text body, and the short links are replaced by
  their original destination URL;
- pixels already delivered stop recording openings after the consent is
  withdrawn, and tracked links stop recording clicks.

The module does not replace the privacy notice: the deployer must still
describe the tracking pixel, its purposes and the retention period in
the privacy policy, and must keep the unsubscribe link in the mailing
footer.

Configuration
=============

After installing the module, no tracking pixel or per-recipient tracked
link is added to a mass mailing unless the recipient granted a tracking
consent.

Record the consents in *Email Marketing > Configuration > Tracking
Consents*, or import them with the usual import tool. Each record has an
email address and a state:

- *Granted*: tracking is allowed;
- *Refused*: tracking is disabled, but the recipient keeps receiving the
  mailings.

The *Consent to Email Tracking* field is also available on mailing
contacts and partners. It is editable and searchable, so it can be used
in mailing domains, filters and imports. The *Manage*/*Open Consent
Record* button opens the related record with its audit trail.

Each mailing has a *Tracking Privacy* tab:

- *Only recipients who gave consent* (default): the pixel and the
  personalised links are added only for email addresses with a recorded
  consent;
- *Never*: no recipient is tracked individually;
- *Always (exemptions only)*: every recipient is tracked. Use it only
  for communications covered by a legal exemption of the Garante
  guidelines, after assessing the legal basis.

The tab also has an *Add Tracking Preferences Link* option. When the
policy is *Only recipients who gave consent*, the footer link is added
to tracked and non-tracked mailings, so a recipient who has not chosen
yet can opt in.

In *Email Marketing > Configuration > Settings* you can set the default
tracking policy and the default footer option for new mailings.

The public page is protected by an opaque HMAC token, without the email
address in the URL. A recipient reaches it from the footer of a mailing.
There, the recipient can stop tracking only, allow tracking again, or
unsubscribe from all emails. The choice is stored and logged, together
with the originating mailing. The token is bound to the mailing trace
and does not expire: links in old emails keep working as long as the
trace is kept, and stop working when the trace is purged. A forwarded
email can be used to change the recipient's choice, so keep the trace
and the consent audit as evidence.

The module deliberately provides no global switch to bypass consent. If
a mailing falls under one of the legal exemptions, select the *Always
(exemptions only)* policy on that mailing or use Odoo's regular email
templates, which do not add a tracking pixel. The module does not assess
the legal basis of a specific mailing: that remains the controller's
decision.

Usage
=====

1. Install the module.
2. Make sure the privacy notice mentions the tracking pixel, its
   purposes and the retention period.
3. Collect the tracking consent, for example through a website form, a
   contract, a newsletter subscription or an import, and record it in
   *Email Marketing > Configuration > Tracking Consents* or directly on
   the contact/partner (*Consent to Email Tracking* field).
4. Check the *Tracking Privacy* tab of the mailing. Keep the default
   policy for ordinary promotional mailings; use *Never* or *Always
   (exemptions only)* only when the legal basis requires it.
5. Send the mailing as usual. Recipients without a granted consent
   receive the same email without the tracking pixel and without tracked
   links: the short links are replaced by their target URL, in HTML and
   plain text.
6. Keep the footer of the mailing, which contains the unsubscribe link
   and, for mailings under the consent policy, the tracking preferences
   link.

Withdrawing the tracking consent only stops the tracking: the recipient
keeps receiving the mailings without the pixel. To stop the emails as
well, the recipient uses the unsubscribe link.
